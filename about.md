# YARM
**YARM (Yet Another Replay Mod)** is a Geometry Dash mod developed by **TheTeamXW**, created to make replaying, recording, and reviewing gameplay easier and more convenient.

## Features

- **Replay recording** — Record your gameplay so you can revisit your runs later.
- **Replay playback** — Watch recorded runs again without having to play through the level yourself.
- **Replay management** — Keep your replays organized and make it easier to find the ones you want to watch.
- **Playback controls** — Get more control over how you review your recorded gameplay.
- **Performance in mind** — Designed to integrate with Geometry Dash while keeping the experience as smooth as possible.
